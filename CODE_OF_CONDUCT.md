# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing and deliberately
disruptive behavior are not welcome. Discuss technical decisions with evidence
and assume good faith while it remains reasonable to do so.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and community interactions
that violate these expectations. Serious incidents should be reported
privately to a maintainer through the contact method listed on the repository
owner's profile.
